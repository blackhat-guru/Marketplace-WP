About Templora

Better websites start from honest templates.

Templora is a marketplace for website templates made by independent designers and developers. We focus on one thing: helping buyers know exactly what they’re getting, and helping creators get paid fairly for good work.

Our mission

Too many template marketplaces bury the details that matter — what pages are included, what the license allows, whether anyone will answer a support question. We put those details first, on every listing.

For buyers

Full-page previews, device previews, clear licenses and documented features before you pay.

Our quality philosophy

Every product is reviewed by a person before it’s published.

  • Previews match the files

    Screenshots and demos must reflect exactly what buyers download.

  • Accessible by default

    Keyboard support, readable contrast and semantic markup are part of review.

  • Performance-minded

    Lean dependencies and optimised assets, so your site starts fast.

  • Clean licensing

    Bundled fonts, images and libraries must be redistributable.

A community of independent creators

Templora is built with feedback from the people who make and use templates every day. Seller and buyer stories will appear here once the marketplace opens — we won’t publish testimonials until they’re real.
