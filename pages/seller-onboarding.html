Who can sell

Independent designers, developers and studios who own the rights to everything in their templates.

  • You created the template, or hold the rights to sell it
  • All bundled fonts, images and libraries are licensed for redistribution
  • You can provide support as described in your store policy
  • You agree to the Seller Agreement

How selling works

From application to your first sale.

    Tell us about your store and the kind of templates you make.

  2. Set up your store

    Add a logo, banner, description and support policy.

  3. Upload a product

    Screenshots, live demo, ZIP, documentation and license options.

  4. Product review

    We check quality, licensing and that the demo matches the files.

  5. Publish & track

    Follow sales, earnings and payouts from your dashboard.

Product review & quality expectations

Reviewers check each submission against the same checklist. If something needs work, you’ll get specific feedback and can resubmit.

  • Responsive and accessible — works from 360px up, keyboard-navigable, sufficient contrast.
  • Clean, valid code — no console errors, no obfuscated scripts, organised assets.
  • Accurate previews — screenshots and live demo match the downloadable files.
  • Documentation — setup, structure and customisation instructions included.
  • Licensing — third-party assets are credited and redistributable.
